# CT-10 Statistical outliers by vendor

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Test type | Risk indicator (analytics flag) |
| Risk rating | Medium |
| Prepared | Generated by `audit_analytics.pipeline` on 2026-11-17 |
| Data | Synthetic accounts-payable ledger (not real company data) |

## Control objective
Payment amounts are consistent with each vendor's usual invoice size.

## Risk
An unusually large payment can signal an error, inflated invoice or a misused vendor account.

## Population
All 5,036 payments posted from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (CAD 53,939,358.33); full population, no sampling. Completeness checks: `outputs/completeness_checks.csv`.

## Procedure
For vendors with at least 20 payments, compute a robust z-score on log(amount) using the median and MAD; flag scores above 3.5. Implemented in Python (src/audit_analytics/analytics.py).

Query / code: `vendor_outliers()` in [`src/audit_analytics/analytics.py`](../src/audit_analytics/analytics.py)

- Amount of flagged payments: CAD 5,563,562.05
- Known-answer check: 5 of 5 planted issues found, 0 missed. Other flags: 24 were planted issues of another type, 4 were clean records.
