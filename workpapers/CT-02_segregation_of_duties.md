# CT-02 Segregation of duties

|---|---|

| Data | Synthetic accounts-payable ledger (not real company data) |

## Control objective
The person who creates a payment cannot also approve it.

## Risk
One person can create and release an unauthorised payment without a second check.

## Population
All 5,036 payments posted from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (CAD 53,939,358.33); full population, no sampling. Completeness checks: `outputs/completeness_checks.csv`.

## Procedure
Flag every payment where created_by equals approved_by.

Query / code: [`sql/control_tests/02_segregation_of_duties.sql`](../sql/control_tests/02_segregation_of_duties.sql)

## Conclusion
Exceptions noted (8). In this synthetic scenario the control did not operate effectively for the flagged payments, pending follow-up.

## Follow-up procedure
Obtain support for each payment and confirm it was for a genuine invoice; check whether the system allowed self-approval or a role was misconfigured.

## Recommended management action
Configure the workflow to block self-approval and review AP Supervisor role access quarterly.
