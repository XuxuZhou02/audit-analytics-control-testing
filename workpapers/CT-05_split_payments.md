# CT-05 Split payments

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Test type | Key control test |
| Risk rating | High |
| Prepared | Generated by `audit_analytics.pipeline` on 2026-11-17 |
| Data | Synthetic accounts-payable ledger (not real company data) |

## Control objective
Purchases are not split into smaller payments to stay under an approval limit.

## Risk
Splitting defeats the delegation of authority while each payment looks compliant.

## Population
All 5,036 payments posted from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (CAD 53,939,358.33); full population, no sampling. Completeness checks: `outputs/completeness_checks.csv`.

## Procedure
Group payments by vendor, day and approver; flag groups of two or more where each payment is within the limit but the total is above it.

Query / code: [`sql/control_tests/05_split_payments.sql`](../sql/control_tests/05_split_payments.sql)

## Conclusion
Exceptions noted (14). In this synthetic scenario the control did not operate effectively for the flagged payments, pending follow-up.

## Follow-up procedure
Obtain the invoices in each group and confirm whether they relate to one purchase.

## Recommended management action
Add a same-vendor, same-day aggregation check to the approval workflow.
