# CT-03 Approval above delegated authority

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Test type | Key control test |
| Risk rating | High |
| Prepared | Generated by `audit_analytics.pipeline` on 2026-11-17 |
| Data | Synthetic accounts-payable ledger (not real company data) |

## Control objective
Payments are approved by someone whose delegated limit covers the amount.

## Risk
Large payments bypass the level of review the delegation of authority requires.

## Population
All 5,036 payments posted from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (CAD 53,939,358.33); full population, no sampling. Completeness checks: `outputs/completeness_checks.csv`.

## Procedure
Join each payment to the approver's limit in the employee table and flag amounts above it.

Query / code: [`sql/control_tests/03_exceeds_approval_authority.sql`](../sql/control_tests/03_exceeds_approval_authority.sql)

## Conclusion
Exceptions noted (6). In this synthetic scenario the control did not operate effectively for the flagged payments, pending follow-up.

## Follow-up procedure
Check whether a higher-level approval was obtained outside the system (e.g. by email) and whether the limit table is current.

## Recommended management action
Enforce limits in the workflow so payments above an approver's limit route to the next level.
