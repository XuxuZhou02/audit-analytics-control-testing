# CT-08 Large payment to new vendor

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Test type | Risk indicator (analytics flag) |
| Risk rating | Medium |
| Prepared | Generated by `audit_analytics.pipeline` on 2026-11-17 |
| Data | Synthetic accounts-payable ledger (not real company data) |

## Control objective
New vendors are validated before large amounts are paid to them.

## Risk
Fictitious vendors are often set up shortly before a large payment.

## Population
All 5,036 payments posted from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (CAD 53,939,358.33); full population, no sampling. Completeness checks: `outputs/completeness_checks.csv`.

## Procedure
Flag payments over $50,000 made within 30 days of the vendor's creation date.

Query / code: [`sql/control_tests/08_new_vendor_large_payment.sql`](../sql/control_tests/08_new_vendor_large_payment.sql)

## Results
- Exceptions: **4** of 5,036 payments (0.08%)
- Amount of flagged payments: CAD 496,547.83
- Known-answer check: 4 of 4 planted issues found, 0 missed. Other flags: 0 were planted issues of another type, 0 were clean records.

## Conclusion
4 payments flagged for follow-up. A flag on a risk indicator is not by itself a control failure; each item needs to be vouched to support.

## Follow-up procedure
Check the vendor onboarding file: who created the vendor, bank-account verification, and whether the creator also handled the payment.

## Recommended management action
Require independent bank-account verification before a new vendor's first payment over $50,000.
