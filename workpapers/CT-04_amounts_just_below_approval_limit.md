# CT-04 Amounts just below approval limit

## Control objective
Payment amounts are not set to avoid a higher level of approval.

## Risk
Amounts set just under a limit can indicate deliberate threshold avoidance.

## Population
All 5,036 payments posted from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (CAD 53,939,358.33); full population, no sampling. Completeness checks: `outputs/completeness_checks.csv`.

## Procedure
Flag payments between 95% and 100% of the approver's limit.

Query / code: [`sql/control_tests/04_just_below_approval_limit.sql`](../sql/control_tests/04_just_below_approval_limit.sql)

## Conclusion
10 payments flagged for follow-up. A flag on a risk indicator is not by itself a control failure; each item needs to be vouched to support.

## Follow-up procedure
Compare each flagged amount with the underlying invoice and purchase order; look for a pattern by creator, approver or vendor.

## Recommended management action
Include near-limit payments in the monthly management review sample.
