# CT-09 Vendor master integrity

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Test type | Key control test |
| Risk rating | High |
| Prepared | Generated by `audit_analytics.pipeline` on 2026-11-17 |
| Data | Synthetic accounts-payable ledger (not real company data) |

## Control objective
Payments go only to vendors that exist in the vendor master and are active.

## Risk
Payments to unknown or deactivated vendors bypass vendor due diligence.

## Population
All 5,036 payments posted from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (CAD 53,939,358.33); full population, no sampling. Completeness checks: `outputs/completeness_checks.csv`.

## Procedure
Left-join payments to the vendor master; flag missing vendors and payments dated after deactivation.

Query / code: [`sql/control_tests/09_vendor_master_integrity.sql`](../sql/control_tests/09_vendor_master_integrity.sql)

## Results
- Exceptions: **6** of 5,036 payments (0.12%)
- Amount of flagged payments: CAD 37,295.44
- Known-answer check: 6 of 6 planted issues found, 0 missed. Other flags: 0 were planted issues of another type, 0 were clean records.

## Conclusion
Exceptions noted (6). In this synthetic scenario the control did not operate effectively for the flagged payments, pending follow-up.

## Follow-up procedure
Trace each payment's bank details and identify how the system accepted the vendor ID.

## Recommended management action
Add a referential-integrity check so payments cannot be released to missing or inactive vendors.
