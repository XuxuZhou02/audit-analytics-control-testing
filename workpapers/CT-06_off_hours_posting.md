## Control objective
Payments are processed during normal, supervised business hours.

## Risk
Payments posted at weekends or late at night are less likely to be reviewed as they happen.

## Population
All 5,036 payments posted from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (CAD 53,939,358.33); full population, no sampling. Completeness checks: `outputs/completeness_checks.csv`.

## Procedure
Flag payments posted on Saturday or Sunday, or between 20:00 and 05:59.

Query / code: [`sql/control_tests/06_off_hours_posting.sql`](../sql/control_tests/06_off_hours_posting.sql)

## Conclusion
15 payments flagged for follow-up. A flag on a risk indicator is not by itself a control failure; each item needs to be vouched to support.

## Follow-up procedure
Check system access logs and whether the work was scheduled (e.g. month-end) and approved.

## Recommended management action
Restrict payment release outside business hours to named users with documented approval.
